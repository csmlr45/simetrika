Privacy policy

Data controller

Simetrika Sàrl, 1 rue Ermesinde, L-1469 Luxembourg.

Personal data contact: contact@simetrika.lu.

Data and purposes

The form provides fields for your name, email address, phone number and project description, to respond to requests for information, a callback or a quote.

Your name, project description and at least one way to contact you (email or phone) are needed to handle your request. Without these details, we cannot respond.

Legal bases

Quote requests and preliminary discussions about a project are based on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Other information requests are based on our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us and following up on their enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Recipients and service providers

Information is accessible to authorised people at Simetrika and, where necessary for their role, technical providers of hosting, email and request delivery services.

Retention

Correspondence is kept for the time needed to answer and follow up on your enquiry. Enquiries that do not lead to further work are deleted as soon as they are no longer needed, and no later than 6 months after the last exchange.

If services are agreed, only information necessary to provide them and comply with legal obligations is kept for the applicable periods. Information needed to defend legal rights in a dispute may be kept for as long as necessary for that purpose.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions of the GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and object to certain processing. Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise your rights, email contact@simetrika.lu, describing your request and providing details that let us identify the relevant correspondence. We will normally respond within one month; any extension permitted by the GDPR will be explained within that period.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD).

Website operation and external services

The current version loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from GitHub. These requests transmit information including your IP address to the providers to display the resources. WhatsApp links open an external service subject to its own privacy policy.

Photographs

Simetrika holds the necessary usage rights for the photographs published on this website.
